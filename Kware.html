About Our School

Serving the Embakasi community with accessible, quality secondary education.

Kware Senior School (formerly known as Kware Secondary School) is a public mixed day secondary school located in Embakasi South Sub-County, Nairobi County, Kenya.

It operates as a C4 Cluster Senior School under Kenya’s Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), hosting learners in Grades 10, 11 and 12.

The school is situated in the Kware neighbourhood (near Pipeline and Mukuru), approximately 9 km east of Nairobi’s Central Business District, and plays an important role in providing secondary education to the local community.

Fees Note (2026)

As a public day senior school (C4), government capitation covers the approved day-school costs. Parents of day senior school learners currently pay no tuition fees under the official Ministry of Education fee structure. Always confirm the latest fee guidelines and any approved levies directly with the school administration.
